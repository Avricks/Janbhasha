Privacy Policy & Child Data Safeguards

LAST REVISED: SEPTEMBER 06, 2026 | VERSION: 3.1 | STATUTORY JURISDICTION: JHARKHAND, INDIA

1. Principles of Data Minimization & Scope

The Janbhasha Platform Initiative is committed to safeguarding the privacy and digital sovereignty of children, educators, and indigenous community members. Our technological architecture is explicitly designed around the principle of edge data minimization: personal identifiers are never requested, stored, or transmitted during pedagogy.

2. Compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023

In strict compliance with Section 9 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023, Republic of India:

  • Zero Tracking or Profiling: We do not engage in behavioral monitoring, commercial profiling, or targeted algorithmic advertising directed toward children.
  • Zero Harmful Processing: No processing is undertaken that causes detrimental effects on the mental or physical well-being of early tribal learners.
  • Verifiable Institutional Consent: Classroom tablet deployments occur under written memoranda with participating district educational authorities and state tribal welfare departments acting as statutory institutional fiduciaries.

3. Local Storage & Edge Audio Ephemerality

Unlike conventional cloud-based educational applications:

  • All student speech recording audio buffers generated during oral pronunciation drills are processed strictly in volatile memory (RAM) by the on-device Conformer acoustic model and immediately purged post-scoring.
  • Raw vocal recordings are never persisted to disk or transmitted across external cellular networks without explicit cryptographic opt-in from academic research partner institutions.
  • Item Response Theory (IRT) assessment scores are indexed against anonymized device node IDs (e.g. NODE_RANCHI_08) rather than student legal names, government IDs, or facial imagery.

4. Telemetry and Aggregate Analytics

Our web portal and administrative dashboards employ strictly privacy-preserving, first-party telemetry:

  • We collect anonymous HTTP transmission latencies, error codes, and aggregated district-level script retention percentages.
  • We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or commercial surveillance SDKs.
  • Cookie preferences can be adjusted at any time via the consent banner or by clearing local storage registers.

5. Information Security & Data Sovereignty

All synchronized data resides exclusively on certified domestic cloud server partitions located within the Republic of India, adhering strictly to Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) data sovereignty guidelines. Local databases on Android hardware utilize AES-256 SQLCipher encryption.
