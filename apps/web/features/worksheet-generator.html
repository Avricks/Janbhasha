Print-First Dual-Script Pedagogical Worksheets

Rural schools frequently suffer power cuts that prevent tablet use during school hours. Janbhasha bridges digital adaptation and paper-based classroom pedagogy by automating dual-script printable exercise generation.

  1. STAGE 01

    DIKSHA / JCERT Competency Mapping

    Select target foundational learning outcomes (FLN) from national and state curriculum taxonomy catalogs.

  2. STAGE 02

    Dual-Script Question Synthesis

    Generates parallel questions in both the indigenous script (e.g. Ol Chiki) and the regional medium (Devanagari/English).

  3. STAGE 03

    High-Contrast Vector Typesetting

    Worksheets render with sharp high-contrast typography optimized for low-cost monochrome school photocopy machines.

  4. STAGE 04

    Offline Export & Print

    Generates compact PDF files under 180 KB that can be printed directly from low-power classroom USB printers.
