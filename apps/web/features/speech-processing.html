Dialect-Resilient Acoustic Conformer Pipeline

Standard commercial speech engines suffer severe failure rates (>65% WER) on tribal dialects due to unmodeled glottal stops, non-pulmonic consonants, and tonal vowel variations. Janbhasha deploys specialized acoustic encoders optimized for Eastern Indian indigenous phonetics.

  1. STAGE 01

    80-Channel Filterbank Capture

    Audio is digitized at 16 kHz mono with adaptive noise suppression filtering rural ambient classroom background noise.

  2. STAGE 02

    Conformer Feature Extraction

    Self-attention and depthwise convolutional blocks capture both macro prosody and micro phonemic transitions.

  3. STAGE 03

    Phonetic Forced Alignment

    Learner speech is time-aligned against reference curriculum transcripts at millisecond precision to isolate hesitation.

  4. STAGE 04

    Oral Fluency Metric Generation

    Generates Words Per Minute (WPM), phoneme substitution penalties, and intelligibility scores for educator review.
