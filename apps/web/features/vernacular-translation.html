Agglutinative Morphology & Script Rendering

Indigenous Austroasiatic languages feature complex agglutinative morphology that conventional tokenizers corrupt. Janbhasha utilizes specialized morpheme-aware byte-pair tokenizers trained on verified tribal literature.

  1. STAGE 01

    Morpheme Boundary Isolation

    Sentence inputs undergo affixes extraction preserving root stems against grammatical inflection errors.

  2. STAGE 02

    Phonetic Syllable Mapping

    Phonological sequences are converted into International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) tokens to ensure accent preservation.

  3. STAGE 03

    Unicode Script Generation

    Direct mapping into official Unicode blocks for Ol Chiki (U+1C50 to U+1C7F) and Warang Citi (U+118A0 to U+118FF).

  4. STAGE 04

    Lexical Validation Check

    Synthesized glosses are verified against the Jharkhand Tribal Research Institute gold standard lexicon.
