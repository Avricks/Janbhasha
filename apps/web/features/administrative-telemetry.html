Differential Privacy & Anonymized Edge Aggregation

Janbhasha safeguards children's constitutional privacy rights by implementing zero personally identifiable information (PII) collection. Only statistical cohort aggregates and dialect phoneme stability indicators leave the school premise.

  1. STAGE 01

    Edge Data Minimization

    Individual student names and biometrics are never stored on tablets. Learners authenticate via randomized physical QR tokens.

  2. STAGE 02

    Differential Privacy Noise Injection

    Mathematical epsilon-differential privacy noise is injected into classroom item responses prior to cloud synchronization.

  3. STAGE 03

    District-Level Cohort Rollup

    Aggregates literacy indicators into cluster-level indicators: Ol Chiki fluency rate, phoneme confusion matrices, and attendance.

  4. STAGE 04

    Longitudinal Script Preservation Index

    Linguists monitor tribal dialect drift, phonetic survival rates, and inter-generational vernacular literacy transfer.
