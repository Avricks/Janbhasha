2. Dual Licensing Framework

Janbhasha operates under a transparent dual licensing architecture designed to balance open public good with enterprise institutional stability:

  • Open Source Community Core: The client-side Android learner application and offline SQLite data models are released under the MIT and Apache 2.0 open-source licenses for community educational non-commercial use.
  • Institutional Managed Services: Enterprise cloud delta synchronization, centralized administrative telemetry rollups, and Conformer acoustic model updates are licensed via institutional Service Level Agreements (SLAs) with district educational authorities.

3. Indigenous Linguistic Sovereignty & Intellectual Property

The Janbhasha Initiative explicitly recognizes the collective intellectual and cultural heritage rights of the Santhal, Munda, and Ho indigenous communities:

  • All traditional folklore, oral narratives, and lexical corpus contributions gathered during field recordings remain the unalienable cultural heritage of the respective indigenous communities.
  • Janbhasha holds no commercial copyright claims over community vernacular vocabulary or script orthographies.
  • Model weights derived from tribal field recordings are dedicated to educational preservation and shall never be locked behind exclusive proprietary commercial paywalls.

4. Institutional Service Level Agreement (SLA)

For government and district deployments under active operational agreements:

  • Edge Availability: 100% offline availability of local curriculum runtime regardless of cellular carrier outages.
  • Cloud Synchronization Gateway: 99.9% uptime for the central delta ledger ingestion cluster.
  • Incident Response: Critical synchronization cluster disruptions investigated within 4 business hours.
