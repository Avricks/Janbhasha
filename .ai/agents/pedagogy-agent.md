## Curriculum Framework

### Proficiency Levels
- **Beginner** (A1): Basic vocabulary and phrases
- **Elementary** (A2): Simple conversations
- **Intermediate** (B1): Fluent conversation
- **Upper Intermediate** (B2): Complex topics
- **Advanced** (C1): Native-like proficiency
